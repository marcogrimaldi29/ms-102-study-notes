MS-102 is retiring: what it means for you

Microsoft is retiring Exam MS-102 and the Microsoft 365 Certified: Administrator Expert certification on November 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM Central Standard Time.

Retirement: November 30, 2026

Still preparing? Sit the exam before the deadline. After it, you can no longer take MS-102 or earn the certification.

Already certified? Your certification stays on your Microsoft Learn transcript, as an active certification, until its current expiry date. If you're eligible to renew before November 30, 2026, Microsoft recommends doing so. Once the certification retires, you can't renew it.

Sources: MS-102 study guide · Administrator Expert certification page · Exam retirement · Credential retirement

The successor: Exam AB-650

Microsoft's June 2026 credentials roundup names Exam AB-650: Administering Microsoft 365 and AI Services as the replacement for MS-102. Passing AB-650 earns Microsoft 365 Certified: Microsoft 365 and AI Services Administrator Associate. No other exam or certification is listed as an MS-102 replacement.

How these notes map to AB-650

AB-650 keeps much of the MS-102 administration core and adds a large Copilot and agents domain. Here's how each page of these notes lines up with the AB-650 skills measured:

These notesRelevance to AB-650
1 · TenantMostly These all reappear: domains, org settings, Service health, network connectivity insights, Microsoft 365 Backup, licensing (including group-based), users, groups, contacts, roles, PIM, administrative units and Graph PowerShell. AB-650 adds tenant branding, licensing for AI services, and managing mailboxes, Teams and SharePoint sites.
2 · IdentityPartly These carry over: authentication methods, SSPR, Password Protection, Conditional Access, Identity Protection and MFA. Directory synchronization (IdFix, Entra Connect Sync / Cloud Sync, Connect Health) isn't listed in the AB-650 skills.
3 · Defender XDRNarrower Only Defender for Office 365 is listed: alerts, threat policies, investigation & response, and attack simulation training. Secure Score and Exposure Management, incidents and advanced hunting, Defender for Endpoint, Defender for Cloud Apps and Threat Intelligence aren't listed as skills. The audience profile only asks for an understanding of Defender XDR capabilities.
4 · PurviewReframed DLP (now including endpoints and Copilot), sensitive info types, sensitivity labels and retention are phrased as identify requirements. AB-650 also adds Data Security Posture Management (DSPM) for monitoring AI activity.
Not covered hereNew The AI services domain (35–40%): Copilot readiness and settings, Copilot Search and connectors, agents and Microsoft Agent 365, Microsoft Entra Agent ID, the agent registry and the Copilot Control System. Use the AB-650 study guide and course AB-650T00 for these topics.

Exam reality check

Expect case studies, drag-and-drop ordering, "build list" sequencing, hotspot (dropdown) and yes/no statement sets. Many questions hinge on which tool / which licence / which order. The Exam Tips page collects the highest-yield caveats. Most questions cover GA features; commonly-used Preview features can appear.
